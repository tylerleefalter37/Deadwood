Action Points & Logistics

Each day starts with 5 Base Action Points (AP) (or 7 once you construct the Frontier Stables). Every wilderness hunt, mine expedition, and timber felling costs 1 AP.

Clicking SLEEP & REST advances to the next dawn. Sleeping consumes 1 Provision and 1 Fresh Water. If you run out of either, you will suffer severe HP dehydration or starvation penalties! (Building a Fresh Water Well permanently solves thirst).

Quick-Draw Combat Mechanics

When you accept a bounty contract, you step directly into the dust at high noon. A heavy tension bell chimes, accompanied by your pounding heartbeat.

Wait for the bell stinger and the red DRAW! signal. If you click or tap spacebar before the signal, you commit a FOUL DRAW (instant misfire, usually resulting in you being gunned down).

Reaction calculation: Effective Time = Raw Milliseconds - Weapon Draw Bonus - Ranch Workbench Bonus. If your reaction is faster than the outlaw's roll, your shot connects cleanly.

Homestead & Outbuildings

Your ranch progresses through 5 tiers from a humble firepit to a sprawling cattle estate. Outbuildings grant permanent logistical buffs: Stone Smokehouse stops meat spoilage, Skinning Shed grants +25% fur sell prices, Gunsmith Bench adds +15ms quickdraw speed, and the Defense Stockade fends off night raids.

Hunting & Mining Logistics

Hunting with a Firearm requires shot and powder; it has higher odds of taking down big game (elk, bear, buck). Hunting with a Longbow is free and silent, yielding cleaner pelts and preserving ammunition, but requires higher tracking finesse.

Prospecting ranges from panning the muddy Rapid Creek silt, to setting up a sluice box, to digging dangerous underground drift shafts with your pickaxe.
