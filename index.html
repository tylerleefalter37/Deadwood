Gunplay & Reflex Mechanics (Core System)

High noon gunfights are decided by pure reflex timing. When stepping into the street against an outlaw, a resonant funeral bell tolls, followed by your tense heartbeat pulse. Keep your finger off the trigger!

Drawing before the signal is a FOUL DRAW (you panic and misfire in the dirt, suffering a devastating wound). When the sharp bell chime rings and DRAW! flashes, strike SPACEBAR or TAP the canvas instantly.

Authentic Pistol Draw Timing Physics

Your effective reaction time is calculated as:

Effective Ms = Raw Human Reaction + Gun Draw Modifier + Modifications - Ranch Buffs

• Draw Delay Penalties (+ms): Crappier, rusty, or heavy cavalry iron (like the starter Rusty .36 Colt or the 4.5 lb Walker Colt) take longer to clear leather, adding milliseconds to your timer and making you slower. In exchange, heavy calibers deliver devastating stopping power multipliers on bounty payouts.

• Quickdraw Advantages (-ms): Lightweight, snub-nosed models and custom hair-triggers shave milliseconds off your draw, making you faster on the strike.

Daily Action Points & Day Logistics

You begin each morning with 5 Base Action Points (AP). Building an Artesian Spring Well increases this to 6 AP, while the Frontier Stables elevate your capacity to 8 AP.

Clicking SLEEP & ADVANCE consumes 1 Provision and 1 Water. It resolves mountain deadfall snares, cures meat (if a smokehouse is present), rerolls the 5-Bounty Wall, and checks for nocturnal rustler raids (prevented by fences & stockades).

Archery & Traditional Hunting

Constructing the Archery Practice Range allows you to train your Archery Discipline across 5 progressive ranks. Hunting with the traditional longbow is silent, consumes zero gunpowder, yields pristine undamaged pelts, and unearths rare pre-historic obsidian artifacts.

Industrial Sluice Operations

Progress your mining claim from a Hand Pan & Rocker Box to a 12-ft Wooden Flume Sluice Box, up to an industrial Hydraulic Long Tom Sluice channeling Rapid Creek current for solid ounces of pure placer gold.
