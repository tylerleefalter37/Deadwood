Action Economy & Sleeping

Each day grants 5 Base Action Points (AP) (expanding to 7 once the Frontier Stables are built). Wilderness hunts, river prospecting, timber felling, and gallery training all consume 1 AP.

Clicking SLEEP & REST advances the dawn, consumes 1 Provision and 1 Water, runs mountain trap lines, checks for meat spoilage, and triggers possible nocturnal predator or rustler attacks (prevented by fences & stockades).

Quickdraw Combat & Reflex Physics

Stepping into the street pits you against notorious outlaws at high noon. A church bell tolls, followed by your pounding pulse.

When the sharp bell stinger sounds and the crimson DRAW! strikes the screen, tap the canvas or press SPACEBAR immediately.

Drawing before the signal is a FOUL DRAW (instant panicked misfire). Your effective draw reaction is calculated as: Raw Milliseconds - Weapon Draw Bonus - Weapon Upgrades - Ranch Workbench Bonus.

Traditional Archery Mastery

Construct the Archery Practice Range at your homestead to hone your archery discipline from Novice (Rank 1) to Master Marksman (Rank 5). Archery hunting is silent, free of gunpowder cost, yields pristine undamaged pelts, and unearths rare prehistoric stone artifacts.

Industrial Sluice Operations

Progress your mining setup from a Hand Pan & Rocker Box to a Wooden Flume Sluice Box, up to a Hydraulic Long Tom Sluice. High-tier sluicing requires timber flumes but processes massive pay-dirt for solid ounces of pure gold.

11 Historic Firearms & Custom Gunsmithing

Visit the Deadwood Gunsmith or use your Ranch Workbench to equip and customize historical revolvers. Install Snub-nosed cuts, Match-grade barrels, Hair triggers, Checkered grips, and Progressive rifling to mold your iron for lightning speed or devastating lethality.
