Reflex Gunplay & Draw Physics (Core Combat)

High noon reflex duels are decided by timing on the millisecond clock. When stepping into the street against an outlaw, a resonant funeral gong tolls, followed by your tense heartbeat pulse. Keep your finger off the trigger!

Drawing before the signal is a FOUL DRAW (you panic and misfire in the dirt, suffering a devastating wound). When the sharp bell chime rings and DRAW! flashes, strike SPACEBAR or TAP the canvas instantly.

• Draw Delay Penalties (+ms): Crappier, rusty, or heavy cavalry iron (like the starter Rusty .36 Colt or the 4.5 lb Walker Colt) take longer to clear leather, adding milliseconds to your timer and making you slower. In exchange, heavy calibers deliver massive stopping power multipliers (up to 3.2x) on bounty payouts.

• Quickdraw Advantages (-ms): Lightweight, snub-nosed models and custom hair-triggers shave milliseconds off your draw, making you faster on the strike.

Expanded Action Points (Up to 10 AP Max)

Gold Measurement: Grams & Troy Ounces

Gold is measured in authentic grams (g) and Troy Ounces (1 Troy Oz = 31.10g). You can smelt 31.1g of raw river placer dust into stamped 1 oz Gold Bullion Bars at the Assayer's Office for premium value.

Homestead Crew Automation & Night Raids

Construct a Bunkhouse to hire Ranch Hands (Wages: $2.50 silver + 1 food/day). Assign them to run your Home Sluice (passive gold), patrol as Guards (+Defense), chop timber, or hunt game. If your Ranch Defense is low, midnight rustlers will raid your stores. If high, your guards gun them down and loot their pockets!
